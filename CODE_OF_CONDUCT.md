# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We as members, contributors and maintainers pledge to make participation in Container Desktop a harassment-free
experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex
characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status,
nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behaviour that contributes to a positive environment:

- Being kind and patient with newcomers; reviewing code, not people.
- Giving and accepting constructive feedback gracefully.
- Focusing on what is best for users of the app and the community.
- Owning mistakes, apologising, and learning from them.

Examples of unacceptable behaviour:

- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention.
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, personal or political attacks.
- Public or private harassment.
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission.
- Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards and may remove, edit or reject
comments, commits, issues and pull requests that do not align with this Code of Conduct, and may temporarily or
permanently ban any contributor for behaviour they deem inappropriate.

Report incidents to **ilessio@aiflowlabs.io**. All reports are reviewed promptly and handled confidentially.
